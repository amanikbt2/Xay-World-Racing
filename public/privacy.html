Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Welcome to Xayrush Racing. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile and web application.

1. Information We Collect

Xayrush Racing is designed to respect user privacy. We minimize data collection to provide you with the best gaming experience.

  • Gameplay Data & Progress: High scores, unlocked karts, unlocked tracks, driver selections, and settings preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage / AsyncStorage.
  • Device & Performance Data: Basic device parameters (such as screen orientation, graphics capabilities, and platform) may be processed locally to optimize game rendering and frame rates.
  • Sensory Data: If enabled, tilt steering utilizes your device's accelerometer. Motion sensor data is processed entirely in real-time on your device and is never transmitted or stored remotely.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any data processed by Xayrush Racing is used exclusively to:

  • Maintain and restore your local game progress and settings.
  • Provide responsive touch and tilt controls during gameplay.
  • Ensure smooth 3D rendering and application stability.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your personal data to third parties. We do not use third-party tracking scripts, analytics telemetry, or invasive advertising networks.

4. Data Storage and Security

All user save data (garage inventory, player progress, settings) remains strictly on your local device. If you clear your browser cache or uninstall the application, locally saved game data may be reset.

5. Children's Privacy

Xayrush Racing is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe child data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
